SO HEN CT v0.4 - HYBRID BROWSER

Cac thay doi trong ban nay:
1. Doi ten hien thi cua cua so thanh "So hen CT".
2. Thanh ben thu gon con 64px, mo rong khi dua chuot vao. Ba nut dieu huong chi hien bieu tuong va co ToolTip.
3. Tab Trinh duyet ho tro tao nhieu tab, dong tab phu, moi tab co Chromium browser rieng.
4. Thanh dia chi tab 1 va tab 2 de trong khi khoi dong; nhan Enter de mo dia chi.
5. Luu lich su URL da truy cap trong SQLite va hien goi y tu lich su khi focus vao thanh dia chi.
6. Them nut zoom +/-, nut vua man hinh (dua zoom ve 100%) cho ca tab 1 va tab 2.
7. Dong lich co trang thai "Da tra lich" trong SQLite duoc hien thi nhan "Da tra hen"; cac o du lieu cua dong nay duoc gach ngang.
8. Them luu thong tin dang nhap theo tung host bang Windows DPAPI CurrentUser. Nguoi dung chu dong nhap va bam Luu; nut Tu dien chi dien form, khong tu dong gui dang nhap.

BAO MAT:
- Mat khau duoc ma hoa bang Windows DPAPI CurrentUser truoc khi luu vao SQLite.
- Chi tai khoan Windows da ma hoa moi giai ma duoc tren may nay.
- Khong tu dong luu mat khau va khong tu dong submit form.
- Website HTTP khong ma hoa mat khau tren duong truyen. Uu tien HTTPS neu he thong benh vien ho tro.
- Khong chia se file appointments.db neu co thong tin dang nhap da luu.

TUONG THICH:
- .NET Framework 4.8 x64
- CefSharp 109.1.110 (Chromium 109, giu tuong thich Windows 7 SP1)
- Giu nguyen thu muc du lieu LocalAppData\PatientAppointmentManager de khong mat lich hen cu.

KIEM THU:
- Da kiem tra XML cua XAML va csproj.
- Da doi chieu ten event trong XAML voi handler trong C#.
- Chua build MSBuild va chua chay thu tren Windows 7. Can chay GitHub Actions truoc khi thay ban dang su dung.
